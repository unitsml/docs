== Exactness and digit metadata

`exact="true"` conversions are computed in exact rational arithmetic (CalConnect 36813, with their lexical decimal attributes parsed exactly; an implementation never routes them through binary floating point. `exact="false"` or absent: the declared precision travels, and the result is never presented as exact. Digit metadata — `multiplicandDigits`, `divisorDigits`, `initialAddendDecimalPlace`, `finalAddendDecimalPlace` — are carried by data layers and never discarded on import.

[example]
*Expected outcome.* Degree Fahrenheit carries factor 5/9 and offset 229835/900 in the register: 32 °F → K is exactly stem:[273.15], and the round-trip returns `"32"`-class exact values. Percent per degree carries the declared `0.5729577951308232087679815481` without an exact marker; its results are published at declared precision, never labelled exact.
