== Terms, definitions and abbreviated terms

linear conversion::
a mapping of the form y = d + (b/c)(x + a) between two units.

affine unit::
a unit whose conversion to its canonical anchor has a nonzero offset (degree Celsius, degree Fahrenheit).

difference conversion::
the conversion of a `Diff`-typed quantity: factor-only (UnitsML 4, I4).

refusal::
a typed error identifying a conversion that this part does not permit to be approximated.
