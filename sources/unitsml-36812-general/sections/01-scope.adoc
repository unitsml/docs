== Scope

This document specifies:

- the procedures of the UnitsML specification register: document numbering, types, lifecycle and amendment rules;
- the normative vocabulary of the series and the requirement language (MUST/SHOULD/MAY);
- the artifact discipline: checksums, editions, and the binding between normative parts and their conformance corpora;
- the release and distribution procedures for the data register and its downstream packages.

This document does not specify any behavior of units, quantities, conversions, expressions or evaluation; those are specified in Parts 2 to 8 and in the companion Plurimath series.
