== Abstract

Semantics over the register (UnitsML 3): dimension vectors are the L0 arithmetic-gating level (derived, never stored); L1 quantities are named concepts whose membership (`quantity_references`), primary designation (UnitsML 3 content rule), and assertion resolution (short → unitsml id → NIST id) gate meaning; declared equivalence classes widen addition/subtraction. Scale classes carry machine-checkable operation legality from `scales.yaml` (e.g. `continuous_interval` forbids ×, ÷, scaling). The difference law with invariants I1–I4: differences are closed under addition, subtraction and factor-only conversion; `Abs − Abs` on a non-ratio scale yields a difference; absolute-vs-difference typing follows provenance, not unit; uncertainties are always differences. Strict gating rejects dimension-compatible but kind-incompatible conversions (Bq↔Hz, Sv↔Gy) unless stated kinds are compatible.
