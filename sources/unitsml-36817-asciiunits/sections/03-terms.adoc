== Terms, definitions and abbreviated terms

terminal::
a registered string the grammar matches as one token: a unit symbol, prefix symbol, registered multi-term or prefixed symbol, or a structural keyword.

extender::
a continuation mark for interrupted unit series; legal only inside an expression.

artifact::
the checksummed `artifacts/unitsml.json` compiled from the grammar; the normative form of this language.

edition::
the recorded grammar revision carried by the artifact.
