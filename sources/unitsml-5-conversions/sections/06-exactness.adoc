== Exactness and digit metadata

`exact="true"` conversions MUST be computed in exact rational arithmetic (UnitsML 2), with their lexical decimal attributes parsed exactly; an implementation MUST NOT route them through binary floating point. `exact="false"` or absent: the declared precision travels; implementations MUST NOT present the result as exact. Digit metadata — `multiplicandDigits`, `divisorDigits`, `initialAddendDecimalPlace`, `finalAddendDecimalPlace` — MUST be carried by data layers and MUST NOT be discarded on import.

[example]
*Expected outcome.* Degree Fahrenheit carries factor 5/9 and offset 229835/900 in the register: 32 °F → K is exactly 273.15, and the round-trip returns `"32"`-class exact values. Percent per degree carries the declared `0.5729577951308232087679815481` without an exact marker; its results are published at declared precision, never labelled exact.
