== Terms, definitions and abbreviated terms

register::
the UnitsDB dataset: the seven collections of Clause 5 at one uniform `schema_version`.

collection::
one of the register's YAML files and its validated content.

denomination::
a currency code: a unit of account that is its own base and admits no conversion factor.

primary quantity reference::
the `quantity_reference` entry flagged `primary: true` for a unit — the kind the unit denotes when none is asserted.

equivalence class::
an L1 grouping declared on quantities; kinds in one class are interchangeable for addition and subtraction.
